Integrations B - the cards stay, but they are closed

Every integration keeps its own card; the card just starts closed. The head carries the icon, the name, the badge with the live diagnostics result and the switch - opening one shows the fields it has today. An integration that is off shows one line saying what it would do, not a set of empty fields. Above the list, one sentence says what the Share button actually does right now.

  • The smallest change of the three: same cards, same markup, one disclosure button per head. Nothing has to be relearnt by anyone who used 3.2.
  • Eight heads fit on a laptop screen without scrolling, and the fields of the one you came for are one click away - not behind a selection.
  • On a phone it stays one column and one screen; no subpage, no back button.
  • Quick share stays a switch per card, so the "only one can win" rule is still a reaction rather than a control. The summary line at the top is what makes it visible.
