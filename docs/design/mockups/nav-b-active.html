Navigation B - the active page, three ways

Everything else is proposal B (nav-b.html): wordmark, three pages, status dot, "..." menu. Only the mark on the page you are looking at changes, and the accent stays the carrier in all three. Each row shows the bar at desktop width and at 375 px, where the labels fall away and the mark has to work under a lone icon.

Today - a filled box with a line under it

The label turns white, a grey box appears behind it and the accent is a 2 px line at the bottom of that box. Two marks for one state, and the box reads as a frame.
