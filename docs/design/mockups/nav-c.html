Navigation C - four pages, and Diagnostics moves into Settings

Diagnostics becomes the third tab of Settings, next to General and Integrations - which is what it already is in everything but the path. That leaves four pages: Clips, Activity, Settings, OBS. They fit into one row on every desktop, and on a phone they fit into the tab bar of proposal A without a "More" behind them.

  • /diagnostics stays a path: the toasts, the diagnostics lines and the README link to it, and it opens Settings with that tab.
  • OBS stays a page of its own because it is where the setup happens; it could become a fourth tab later, and then three pages are left.
  • The phone bar has no "More", so the storage warning keeps a dot in the top bar and the version moves to Settings › Diagnostics, where it is anyway.
