Integrations A - a list, and one detail beside it

Every integration is one line: the icon, the name, what it is doing right now, and the live result of its diagnostics check. Only the line you pick opens its fields, in the pane on the right; on a phone that pane is a subpage of its own. Quick share is a radio column over the storage list instead of eight switches that have to talk each other out of it.

  • The page is as long as the list, not as long as every field of every integration - eight lines instead of a screen and a half of scrolling.
  • "Which one does the Share button use?" is answered by a column of radio buttons, and the rule that only one can win is the control itself, not a hidden reaction.
  • The diagnostics dot sits in the line: green, amber or red per integration, with the reason under the name. What is broken is visible without opening anything.
  • Costs a click before the first field, and needs a second view on a phone - the only proposal here that has two screens instead of one.
  • It also needs room: a list beside a form does not fit in the 720 px column the other pages use, so the Integrations tab would be 1040 px wide - the only page in the UI that breaks out of it.
