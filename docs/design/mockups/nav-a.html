Navigation A - a tab bar at the bottom on a phone

The top bar keeps its five pages on a desktop and gains an icon in front of every label. Below 600 px the pages move into a fixed bar at the bottom of the screen: Clips, Activity, Settings and More. "More" opens a sheet with OBS, Diagnostics, the keyboard shortcuts and the numbers that used to sit in the top right: encoder, storage, version, service state.

  • The three pages a session really uses sit under the thumb, and nothing scrolls sideways.
  • The status dot stays in the top bar. The storage warning rides on "More" as an amber dot, so it is in sight without taking a slot of its own.
  • The bar is 56 px plus the safe area and the page reserves that much at the bottom, so it never covers the share row or the timeline.
