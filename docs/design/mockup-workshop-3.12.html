Focus, ARIA and what changed in base.css

Focus and ARIA

The caption lane is a role="listbox" of blocks; every block is a button whose accessible name is the line's text plus its time (“Warte, warte, warte … , 0:20.3 to 0:25.4”). One block is in the tab order at a time (tabindex="0", the rest -1); ←→ move the selection, Alt+arrow moves the focused edge by 0.1 s, Shift+Tab switches which edge has focus. Handles are aria-hidden: they are the pointer's way to the same thing the buttons do, and nothing here is drag-only.

The loaded-chip is aria-live="polite": loading announces “Cut ‘Drei mit einem Schuss’ loaded, workshop mode”. Progress rows are role="status", announced once and not per percent. The focus ring stays --focus, never the accent, so it is visible on the amber button. Exactly one .btn-primary per view.
